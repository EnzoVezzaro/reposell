# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary**: Developers who want to monetize their repos without platform lock-in. They build tools, libraries, CLI utilities, or devops scripts and want to earn directly from users.

**Secondary**: Open source maintainers who want sustainable funding without sponsorship platforms taking a cut.

**Tertiary**: Engineering teams wanting internal listing for internal tools.

**Context**: They're technical, skeptical of marketing, and want proof over claims. They read docs on GitHub, in IDEs, and in browser. They value speed, clarity, and cryptographic guarantees.

## Product Purpose

reposell CLI turns any Git repository into a sellable product with zero configuration. It generates `/sell` endpoints, integrates with Stripe, automates releases via GitHub Actions, and provides cryptographic identity via Ed25519.

It's the developer infrastructure for owning your distribution and revenue.

## Positioning

**Your repo, your keys, your revenue.** Own your tools, own your customers, own your revenue logic. No platform intermediary.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, mechanical, opinionated.** Not cold-corp minimalism. Not editorial magazine. Terminal at 2am meets sound design.

**Reference (user-named):** [soundcn.xyz](https://www.soundcn.xyz) — warm-orange shadcn/ui energy: stone neutrals, playful motion, developer-tool warmth. All three reposell sites share this visual language.

## Anti-references

- Stripe/Linear minimalism (too cold)
- Editorial magazine aesthetics (Klim-influenced, italic serif)
- AI slop gradients, warm cream backgrounds, tiny tracked uppercase labels
- Monospace-as-costume for "technical"

## Design Principles

1. **Soundcn-inspired**: shadcn/ui primitives, warm orange signal, stone neutrals, expressive typography
2. **Warm dark mode**: `#0c0a09` background, not blue-gray
3. **Protocol-first**: Code blocks and cryptographic notation are heroes
4. **Dense but breathes**: Developer scanning, not marketing reading
5. **Motion as state**: Verify success, copy complete, not delight for delight's sake
6. **Sound design**: Web Audio synth for interactions (like soundcn)
